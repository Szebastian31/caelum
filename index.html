Caelum

Hold your phone up and the real sky over you lines up behind the glass. Every star is calculated for this exact minute, and the app tells you how accurate the compass is.

  • Here on the Mac: drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • On a phone: tap the motion button and point it at the sky.
  • Drag the time ruler to fast-forward the night, and tap any star or planet.
  • Find something and an arrow leads you to it, with a tick when it's centred.

A sibling to Consensus Weather, Fidelis and Vestis

How accurate is this?

Star and planet positions are calculated on your phone for the current minute and are good to about a third of a degree, smaller than the Moon. The weak point is the phone's compass: it's usually off by 5 to 15° and worse near metal or magnets. The badge at the top shows how far off it thinks it is. Moving the phone in a figure 8 recalibrates it.
